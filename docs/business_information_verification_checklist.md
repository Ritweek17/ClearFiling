# Public Business Information Verification Checklist

**Audit date:** 2026-11-14  
**Scope:** Read-only review of the current public website source, including rendered page content, central data, legal copy, service/resource content, SEO metadata and structured data. No external sources were used to substitute for owner confirmation. No `.env` file was read and no source content was changed.

## Executive summary

The project brief confirms the public brand **ClearFiling**, legal business name **Clear Filing & Compliance LLP**, three physical office cities (**Delhi, Deoria, Gorakhpur**) and online service availability across India. The source instead uses **“ClearFiling and Compliance LLP”** as the legal name. It publishes specific street addresses, landmarks, postal codes, geo-coordinates, contact channels, operating hours, response commitments, professional credentials, history, client/filing/satisfaction metrics, client names and testimonials that are not confirmed by the supplied brief.

The website has several overlapping business-data sources. `client/src/config/business.js` and `client/src/config/contact.js` claim to be central sources, but `client/src/data/companyInfo.js`, `client/src/data/footerConfig.js`, `client/src/components/features/StatsStrip.jsx`, `client/src/pages/About.jsx`, and components/service content also hardcode public facts. This means correcting one source may leave conflicting claims elsewhere.

**No facts below are independently validated.** “Verified project information” means only information explicitly supplied in the project brief; it does not mean independently verified against corporate, professional or contact records.

## Architectural assumption — public document delivery

The Admin Dashboard manages only **Public Documents** and **Draft Documents** (sample draft letters and document formats). Both are intended for public access when published. There is no confidential client-document storage requirement for this feature. This owner-supplied policy is recorded as an architectural assumption for this checklist; it does not request or imply a Cloudinary storage redesign.

## Discrepancies found in the current source

1. **Legal name:** project brief says `Clear Filing & Compliance LLP`; business config, legal policies, page copy, HTML author/keywords and JSON-LD say `ClearFiling and Compliance LLP`.
2. **Repeated sources:** contact values and office addresses appear in `config/contact.js`, `data/officeLocations.js`, `data/companyInfo.js`, `data/footerConfig.js`, legal copy and page-level literals. Updating a central file alone will not update every displayed copy.
3. **Metrics are independently hardcoded:** `business.js` says 10,000+ clients and filings, 99.4%, five professionals, 6+ years and 60+ services; several UI components separately restate subsets. Values may drift.
4. **Average rating mismatch:** six visible testimonials each display five stars, but the summary says 4.9/5. The rating denominator/source is not shown.
5. **History needs a defined date:** 2020 is presented as firm founding/establishment and also used to calculate experience. The source does not establish whether it is LLP incorporation date or earlier practice start.
6. **Service scope needs contextual qualification:** source describes online all-India coverage, says all services are digital/no visit required, and contains service-specific assertions. The project brief establishes nationwide online service availability but does not verify every service’s state coverage or eliminate in-person/document requirements.
7. **Schema fans out the same unverified facts:** JSON-LD publishes street addresses, coordinates, hours, emails, phone numbers, founding date and legal name. Corrections must cover schema as well as visible page content.
8. **Office cities align; precise places do not:** source has exactly three offices for the brief’s cities, but labels Delhi as headquarters and gives full addresses/coordinates that the brief does not confirm.

## Recommended source-of-truth files

These are recommendations for a later approved implementation; no source file was changed in this audit.

- **Brand, legal name, founding date and approved company facts:** use `client/src/config/business.js` as the primary authoritative business profile after the owner signs off. Avoid parallel values in `client/src/data/companyInfo.js`.
- **Phones, WhatsApp, email, hours and response commitments:** use `client/src/config/contact.js` as the only contact data source. Replace the hardcoded copies in `footerConfig.js`, `companyInfo.js`, pages and components with references to it.
- **Office cities, addresses, phones per office and structured location fields:** use `client/src/data/officeLocations.js` as the single office registry. Generate footer, contact cards, company info and JSON-LD from it; do not duplicate address text.
- **Metrics, service count and credentials:** store only owner-approved, evidence-backed values centrally or keep them out of marketing copy. Do not make every card maintain a separate number.
- **Service offering claims:** `client/src/data/services.js` is the current catalogue source. Owner-approved scope and professional review should be tied to records/pages; do not assume the catalogue itself proves authorization or current availability.
- **Legal policy text:** `client/src/data/legalContent.js` needs one approved legal-entity reference and separate legal review; it should consume the canonical identity/contact config where possible.
- **SEO and structured data:** `client/src/config/seo.js` and `client/src/components/seo/SchemaHelpers.js` should derive identity/address/contact from the approved source-of-truth files rather than introduce another set of facts. `client/index.html` should remain a homepage fallback, not a separate source for legal identity.
- **Testimonials and client names:** retain in dedicated data modules only after written permission/owner confirmation; `TestimonialsSection.jsx` and `data/topClients.js` are current sources.
